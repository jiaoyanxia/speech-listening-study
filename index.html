Listening in background sound

You will hear short sentences with background sounds. After each sentence, enter the five words you heard and rate how much effort listening required.

Before you begin
  • Use headphones in a quiet room.
  • Keep the same volume throughout the study.
  • Each sentence can be played once.

Audio setup

Set a comfortable volume

Play the sample and adjust your device volume. The sound should be clear and comfortable. Do not change the volume after continuing.

Instructions

What to do after each sentence

  1. Press Play sentence and listen once.
  2. Enter five words you heard. Guess if you are unsure.
  3. Rate your listening effort, then continue.

The response controls appear after the audio finishes, so you can focus on listening.
