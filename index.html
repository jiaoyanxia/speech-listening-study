Listening in background sound

You will hear short sentences with background sounds. After each sentence, type any words you heard and rate how much effort listening required.

Before you begin
  • Use headphones in a quiet room.
  • Keep the same volume throughout the study.
  • Each sentence can be played once.

Study information and consent

Consent to participate

Study title

Speech Recognition and Listening Effort in Background Sound

Researcher

Yixuan Jin, MA in Media Arts, University of Michigan

Purpose of the study

This study examines how different types of background sound affect speech recognition and listening effort.

What you will do

You will complete a brief questionnaire about your language and hearing background. You will then listen to short English sentences presented with background sound, type the words you heard, and rate your listening effort after each sentence. The study takes approximately 20–25 minutes.

Risks or discomforts

You may experience mild listening fatigue, frustration, or discomfort from listening to speech in background sound. Set your device to a comfortable volume before beginning. You may take a break before the main study or stop participating at any time.

Benefits

You may not receive a direct benefit from participating. The results may help researchers understand speech perception and listening effort in different background sound conditions.

Privacy and confidentiality

Do not enter your name, email address, student ID, or other identifying information. Your questionnaire and listening responses will be linked using a participant ID that you create. The website does not automatically transmit your responses. At the end, you may download a results file and send it to the researcher.

Voluntary participation

Participation is voluntary. You may skip any response that is not required for eligibility, stop the study, or close the browser without penalty. Before sending your downloaded results to the researcher, the data remains in your browser.

Questions

For questions about the study, contact Yixuan Jin using the contact information provided in the recruitment message.
